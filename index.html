NeuroSense
Automated Muscle Fasciculation Monitoring

A Python-based tool for detecting and quantifying muscle fasciculations in ultrasound video sequences using adaptive, Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM)-based foreground segmentation.

01 / Overview

Making muscle activity easier to quantify

Motor neuron disease (MND) involves progressive degeneration of motor neurons, resulting in muscle weakness and wasting. Sensitive markers of neuromuscular change are important for supporting early diagnosis and measuring disease progression.

Ultrasound provides a non-invasive, real-time view of muscle. NeuroSense applies adaptive foreground detection to ultrasound image sequences to identify brief, involuntary twitch events known as fasciculations.

01 — PURPOSE

Objective detection

Supports objective detection and quantification of muscle fasciculations in ultrasound videos.

02 — APPROACH

Adaptive foreground segmentation

Models pixel-level motion over time to distinguish background motion from activity associated with fasciculations.

03 — DESIGN

Flexible research platform

Developed in Python to support extensibility, customization and future computer-vision methods.

02 / Method

From MATLAB GMM to Python and OpenCV

The original system used Gaussian mixture models in MATLAB. NeuroSense migrates this approach to Python using OpenCV's BackgroundSubtractorMOG2, which is also GMM-based.

Core processing idea

Ultrasound videos are processed by modelling pixel-level motion across frames. Adaptive background subtraction identifies moving foreground regions that may correspond to muscle activity.

The software was designed to work across a range of skeletal muscles and to be resistant to variations in ultrasound probe orientation—an important consideration in clinical workflows.

Why the transition?

  • Scalability: access to Python's computer-vision and machine-learning ecosystem.
  • Customization: greater control over model parameters for domain-specific requirements.
  • Community support: shared resources, ongoing development and a broad user community.
